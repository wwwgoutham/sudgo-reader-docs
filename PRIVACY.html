Sudgo Reader — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 17, 2026

1. Scope

Sudgo Reader is a private internal-use application operated by Sudgo Incorporated ("Operator") for accessing Operator's own QuickBooks Online accounting data. This Privacy Policy describes what data Sudgo Reader accesses and how that data is handled.

2. Data Accessed

Sudgo Reader accesses Operator's QuickBooks Online accounting data via Intuit's authorized Developer API, including chart of accounts, general ledger transactions, account balances, and similar financial records. Sudgo Reader does not access any data outside of Operator's own QuickBooks Online files.

3. Authentication

Sudgo Reader authenticates to QuickBooks Online using the OAuth 2.0 authorization framework provided by Intuit. Authentication tokens are stored locally on the operator's authorized device and are revoked immediately following each access session.

4. Data Storage

Data retrieved from QuickBooks Online is stored only on the operator's authorized local device for the duration necessary to perform internal accounting tasks. No data is uploaded to third-party servers, transmitted to external parties, or retained beyond what is required for Operator's internal bookkeeping and tax-preparation purposes.

5. Third-Party Sharing

Sudgo Reader does not share, sell, or transmit accounting data to any third party. Data exchange occurs solely between Intuit's QuickBooks Online API and the operator's authorized local device.

6. Users

The sole user of Sudgo Reader is an authorized corporate officer of Operator. Sudgo Reader has no external end users and collects no personal data from any party other than Operator's authorized officer.

7. Changes

Operator may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The Effective Date above will reflect the most recent revision.
